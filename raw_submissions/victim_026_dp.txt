**PROBLEM STATEMENT**

Aryan is given two positive integers `n` and `m`, and he has to construct a divine tree with `n` nodes such that the total divineness of the tree is `m`, i.e., `∑i=1nd(i)=m`. If no such tree exists, Aryan must report that it is impossible.

**LIMITS**

- `1 ≤ n ≤ 10^6`
- `1 ≤ m ≤ 10^12`
- The sum of `n` over all test cases does not exceed `10^6`
